Abstract
We study the density of smooth external forces for which the three-dimensional Navier–
Stokes equations lose classical regularity by a prescribed time T > 0. Starting from the
compact forced blowup solution of OpenAI, we construct a blowup solution near every
given smooth solution while preserving its initial velocity. A smooth cutoff of a local vector
potential makes the given velocity vanish near the support of a rescaled blowup solution.
The two velocities then have no nonlinear interaction, and the resulting force remains smooth
through the blowup time. For fixed viscosity and zero initial velocity, such forces are dense
in the relative L1
tHs
x topology on both T3 and R3 exactly when s < 1/2.
Keywords: forced Navier–Stokes equations; finite-time blowup; force-space density; Sobolev
spaces; compact support.
