A short proof of Helson’s conjecture
Oﬁr Gorodetsky, Mo Dick Wong
Abstract
Let α: N →S1 be the Steinhaus multiplicative function: a completely multiplicative function such
that (α(p))p prime are i.i.d. random variables uniformly distributed on the complex unit circle S1. Helson
conjectured that E| P
n≤x α(n)| = o(√x) as x →∞, and this was solved in a strong form by Harper. We
give a short proof of the conjecture using a result of Saksman and Webb on a random model for the zeta
function.
